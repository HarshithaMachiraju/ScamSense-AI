      Student & Family Scam Defense Guide

      Key rules to identify and neutralize scams targeting students and fresh graduates

      🎓 1. Internship & Job Offer Scams

      Scammers send fake offer letters using brand names (Google, Microsoft, Amazon) demanding "laptop security deposits" or "training kit charges".

      • ❌ Red Flag: Demanding refundable registration fee or laptop caution deposit via UPI.
      • ❌ Red Flag: Recruiter contacting from a personal @gmail.com address or Telegram.
      • ✔️ Rule: Legitimate companies never charge candidates. Verify with your campus placement cell.

      📜 2. Advance-Fee Scholarship Scams

      Fraudulent schemes inform students they won merit grants, requiring an upfront "clearance fee" or "disbursement code charge".

      • ❌ Red Flag: Paying ₹500–₹1,500 to "activate" a ₹50,000 scholarship.
      • ❌ Red Flag: Demanding Aadhaar and bank passbook photos over WhatsApp.
      • ✔️ Rule: Authentic scholarships are listed on official portals (e.g. scholarships.gov.in) and never require payment.

      ⚖️ 3. "Digital Arrest" & Police Threat

      Imposters posing as Police, CBI, or Customs claim an illegal parcel was found in your name and demand a money transfer to avoid arrest.

      • ❌ Red Flag: Video calling on Skype claiming you are under "digital arrest".
      • ❌ Red Flag: Demanding immediate funds transfer to a "Supreme Court verification account".
      • ✔️ Rule: There is no legal procedure called "digital arrest". Hang up immediately and dial 1930.

      📲 4. UPI Reversal & Task Fraud

      Deceptive prompts claiming you won cashback or part-time tasks paying ₹5,000/day for liking YouTube videos.

      • ❌ Red Flag: "Enter your UPI PIN to receive money in your account".
      • ❌ Red Flag: Joining Telegram channels with "prepaid merchant tasks".
      • ✔️ Rule: You NEVER enter a PIN to receive money. PIN is exclusively for authorizing deductions.
